# CliDent — Diseño gráfico, cercano y luminoso

## Palette

Ink `#142e41`, ice `#f3f6f9`, orange `#f07744`, orange text `#b94720`, mint `#d9efea`, muted `#526576`, rule `#d6e0e6`, white `#ffffff`.

## Typography

Outfit in geometric, bold headings and controls; Plus Jakarta Sans in body copy. Hero uses two centered uppercase lines (62–110 px on desktop, 40–60 px on mobile). Other headings use 40–60 px, cards 18–20 px, readable body text and form inputs.

## Structure

Large typography above a horizontal duo of real clinic and team photographs. Treatment explorer uses native disclosure panels and accessible filters. Navy section frames an interactive photo gallery and the clinic's verified facilities. Coverage lookup, exterior photograph and location precede an orange consultation area. A large CliDent wordmark anchors the navy footer.

## Shapes and motion

Rounded photo frames (16–22 px), treatment panels (14 px) and pill controls. Layout uses max-width 1264 px, 112/72/48/40 px viewport deductions and responsive breakpoints at 1100, 800, 600 and 360 px. One photo entrance uses clip-path; all motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. No autoplay gallery or hidden content entrance.

## Behavior and accessibility

Keyboard-focus outlines, skip link, labeled form fields, live filter result count, live gallery index, native disclosures, selected filter state and explicit WhatsApp handoff. No false booking confirmation, records saved or automatic message sending. Logo and photography preserve real clinic identity.
